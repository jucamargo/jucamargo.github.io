Welcome!

Adjunct Professor · School of International Relations, Getulio Vargas Foundation (FGV RI)

I am an applied economist working on education, development, labour markets and environmental political economy in Brazil and Latin America. My research combines randomised field experiments, survey experiments and quasi-experimental designs built on administrative and satellite data.

I hold a PhD in Economics from the São Paulo School of Economics (FGV EESP). I am a research fellow at the Center for Applied Microeconomics (C-Micro/FGV) and at FGV EESP Clear, and I work as a consultant in applied microeconomics for the Inter-American Development Bank.
